Privacy Policy for Lisan Tigrayit

Last updated: 17 May 2026

Lisan Tigrayit ("the app") is a free educational app that helps children and families learn the Tigre (Tigrayit) language of Eritrea. This page explains what data the app does and does not collect. The app is designed to be safe for children under 13 and complies with Google Play's Families policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No data leaves your device.

Information the App Stores on Your Device

To make the learning experience work, the app saves a small amount of information in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on the device. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or any third party:

  • Your learning progress (levels completed, scores, stars).

You can erase all of this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, or by uninstalling the app.

Personal Information

We do not ask for, collect, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Third Parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, social network plugins, or tracking technologies. The app does not contact any external server during normal use.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information and transmits no data off the device, it complies with COPPA and the Google Play Families policy. Parents who have questions or concerns can contact us at the address below.

Data Security

Because nothing leaves the device, there is no server-side data for an attacker to access. Data stored locally is protected by the operating system's normal app sandboxing.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
